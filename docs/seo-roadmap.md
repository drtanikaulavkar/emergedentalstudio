- Ask genuine patients for Google reviews consistently after suitable appointments.

### 8. Review Search Console after data finishes processing

The site-wide indexing and performance reports were still processing during the latest check. When data becomes available, review:

- Search queries
- Pages receiving impressions and clicks
- Average position and click-through rate
- Indexing exclusions
- Mobile and desktop Core Web Vitals

Use the findings to improve pages already receiving impressions before creating large amounts of new content.

### 9. Build legitimate local authority

Seek accurate citations and editorially earned links from relevant sources such as:

- Dental and professional associations
- Local community or neighborhood organizations
- Reputable healthcare directories
- Genuine partners, suppliers, events, or educational collaborations
- Local press when there is a real newsworthy story

Avoid paid link packages, mass directory submissions, private blog networks, and keyword-stuffed guest posts.
